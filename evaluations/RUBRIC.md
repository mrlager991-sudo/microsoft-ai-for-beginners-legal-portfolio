# Portfolio readiness rubric

This rubric evaluates publication readiness of the repository, not course performance.

| Dimension | Points | Evidence expected |
|---|---:|---|
| Claim accuracy and bounded conclusions | 25 | Claims are classified; self-declaration, artifact, test, and assessment are distinct; no credential or grade implication. |
| Evidence access and navigation | 20 | A reviewer reaches each material claim's source within three clicks; machine paths resolve. |
| Reproducibility and tests | 20 | Clean standard-library checks run in one command; saved results match deterministic execution. |
| Legal relevance | 15 | Each demonstration maps a technical concept to a meaningful legal workflow and states the human role. |
| Authorship and AI transparency | 10 | Human direction, AI implementation, prior materials, dates, and limits are specific. |
| Responsible use, privacy, and IP | 10 | Synthetic/public-safe inputs, privacy gates, legal review, attribution, and license treatment are clear. |

Publication requires at least 85/100, all automated checks passing, and zero critical blockers. The number must always be called a **portfolio readiness score**. Only the independent recruiter-style QA may assign the final score and PASS decision.
