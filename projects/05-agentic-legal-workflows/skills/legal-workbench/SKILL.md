# Legal Workbench

Identify jurisdiction, document type, parties, purpose, deadline, and the requested depth. Extract facts with source pointers before analysis. For current law, use verified primary sources and record the access date. Separate text, legal rule, analysis, uncertainty, and recommendation. Mark assumptions and missing facts. Draft changes so a reviewer can see their effect. Do not send, sign, file, accept terms, or make another external commitment without explicit authorization. Do not process client secrets or personal data in an unapproved environment. A qualified human must approve every substantive legal output.
