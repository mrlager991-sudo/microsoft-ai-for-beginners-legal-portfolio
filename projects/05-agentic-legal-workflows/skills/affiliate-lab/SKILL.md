# Affiliate Lab

Treat this as a supplemental cross-functional workflow. Record the offer, audience, channel, constraints, and success metric. Separate observed campaign data from hypotheses. Draft testable creative variants without false urgency, unsupported outcomes, or hidden conditions. Compare results only after checking spend, sample size, tracking quality, and test duration. Respect platform rules, consumer protection, privacy, and brand approval. A human approves publication and spending.
