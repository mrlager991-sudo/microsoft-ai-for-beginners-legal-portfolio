# Evidence Brief

Confirm the question, decision, timeframe, and acceptable source types. Prefer primary and authoritative sources. Separate sourced fact, inference, and unknown. Link each material claim to its source and state meaningful conflicts or gaps. Compare options using the user's actual criteria. End with a bounded recommendation and identify what a human must decide. Do not invent citations, silently broaden the task, or treat legal information as a specialist opinion. Minimize personal data and stop if handling the supplied material would exceed the authorized scope.
